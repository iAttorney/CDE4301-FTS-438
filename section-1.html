1. Introduction

1.1 Formula SAE Competition

Formula SAE (FSAE) is an intercollegiate student design competition organized by SAE International where university teams from around the world design, manufacture, build, and race formula-style race cars. Cars are judged based on design, cost, efficiency, and performance through a series of dynamic and static events. Team members will also be judged on their capabilities in team management, design thinking, technical knowledge, and engineering practices.

1.2 NUS Formula SAE EV Team and Competition History

National University of Singapore (NUS) FSAE ventured into the Electric Vehicle (EV) category in 2022 and had the highest placing in 2025 with 7th overall. The most recent competition in 2026 saw the team place 39th due to unforeseen technical failures.
