2. Design Statement and Value Proposition

2.1 Design Statement

To design the aerodynamic package of the R27e so that it raises the car’s full-car aerodynamic efficiency (CL/CD) in cornering from 2.27 to at least 2.40 without reducing full-car downforce, and to make the team’s CFD simulations more reliable, in support of the team’s goal of a top-10 finish in 2027.

2.2 Value Proposition

Improving total downforce would add traction to the car in corners and improve lap times and competition score.

Improving our simulation allows us to be more confident in our design and make more educated decisions.
