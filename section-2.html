2. Design Statement and Value Proposition

2.1 Design Statement

To design the aerodynamic package of the R27e so that it raises the car’s full-car aerodynamic efficiency (CL/CD) in cornering from 2.27 to at least 2.40 without reducing full-car downforce, and to make the team’s CFD simulations more reliable, in support of the team’s goal of a top-10 finish in 2027.

2.2 Value Proposition

Greater downforce increases the grip available to the tyres in corners, allowing higher cornering speeds and shorter lap times. Because the endurance event, which carries the most points, is dominated by corners (Section 3.2), these gains translate directly into a higher competition score. Improving efficiency at the same time ensures that the extra downforce does not come at the cost of straight-line speed or energy consumption. More reliable CFD simulations, in turn, allow the team to distinguish genuine design improvements from numerical noise, so that design decisions this year and in future years rest on trustworthy data.
